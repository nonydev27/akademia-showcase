What Akademia Does

Everything your school needs,
nothing it doesn't

Built around how Ghanaian schools actually work — not a generic template adapted for Africa.

Multi-School Architecture

Each school runs as a fully isolated tenant. Student records, fees, and grades from one school are never visible to another. One platform, many schools, zero data leaks.

Role-Based Access Control

Three clear roles — Platform Admin, School Admin, and Staff (Teachers). Each person sees exactly what they need: teachers only access their assigned classes and subjects.

Student Records

Admission numbers, personal details, guardian contacts, class enrolments, and photo uploads. Bulk CSV import for large intakes. Clean admission codes (e.g., VIS-001).

Daily Attendance

Mark students Present, Absent, or Tardy per day. Admins get a bird's-eye attendance overview. Teachers only see the classes assigned to them — no accidental edits.

Fee Management

Define fee structures per term. Track charges and payments per student. Paystack integration for online payments with webhook + polling verification. Auto-notifications when fees are cleared.

Multi-Component Grading

CA (30%), Mid-term (20%), End-of-term Exam (50%) — computed automatically. Bulk grade entry per class. Finalization lock so grades can't be changed after closing. Configurable grade bands.

Report Cards & PDF Generation

Auto-generated PDF report cards per student per term. Cards are held in WITHHELD state until all fees are cleared — the Result-Fee Intercept. Released cards are sent to guardians automatically.

Email & SMS Communications

Templated messages sent over Email (Resend) and SMS (Arkesel — Ghana numbers). Notify parents about attendance, fee receipts, and report card releases. Every dispatch is logged.

Subscriptions & Licensing

Per-school subscription with configurable grace periods. Expired-but-in-grace schools get read-only access. Fully expired schools are locked out — and can renew through Paystack directly in the app.

Human-Readable Codes

No UUIDs shown to users. Every entity has a clean, meaningful code — school initials (VIS), admission numbers (VIS-001), teacher access codes (T-AO-ENGLP2-JHS2A). Professional and intuitive.

Audit Log

Every significant action records an actor, action type, target, and timestamp — giving schools a tamper-evident administrative history for accountability and compliance.

Native Desktop App (Tauri)

Packaged as a native Windows .exe installer via Tauri. Works offline for core functions. No browser required — a real, installable desktop application. Coming soon to Microsoft Store.

How It Works

From setup to running
in under an hour

1

School registers and gets onboarded

The Platform Admin creates a school tenant with a unique code (e.g., VIS for Vilaworld International School), sets the subscription plan, and creates the School Admin account. The school is live immediately.

2

Admin sets up classes, subjects, and staff

The School Admin creates academic years, terms, class divisions (JHS 2A, JHS 2B, SHS 1…), subjects, and staff accounts. Teachers are assigned specific class-subject combinations so they only see what's relevant to them.

3

Students are enrolled (bulk or one by one)

Import students from a CSV or add them individually. Akademia assigns clean admission numbers automatically. Guardians are linked to students for notifications. Students are enrolled into classes per term.

4

Daily operations begin — attendance, grades, fees

Teachers mark attendance each morning. Grades are entered at end of assessment periods. Fee payments are recorded as they come in. Everything updates in real-time across the admin dashboard.

5

Report cards generated and released

At end of term, the Admin publishes results. The Result-Fee Intercept holds cards for students with outstanding fees. Once cleared, PDFs are generated and dispatched to guardians via email and SMS automatically.

The Result-Fee Intercept

The most powerful financial tool in Akademia. Report cards are automatically withheld until a student's outstanding fee balance reaches zero. No manual tracking. No awkward conversations. The system handles it.

The moment a payment clears through Paystack, the card is released — the PDF is generated, uploaded, and the guardian is notified by both email and SMS. Instantly.

FAQ

Questions we've been asked

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Akademia is built specifically for Ghanaian Primary, JHS (Junior High School), and SHS (Senior High School) schools. The grading system, curriculum structure, terminology, and integrations (Paystack for fees, Arkesel for SMS) are all tailored to how Ghanaian schools operate — not a generic global template adapted for Africa.
When grades are finalized for a term, Akademia generates a report card PDF for each student and places it in a WITHHELD state. It stays withheld until the student's full fee balance for that term is paid. The moment a payment is verified through Paystack (via webhook), the system automatically releases the card — generates the PDF, uploads it to storage, and sends the guardian a download link by both email and SMS. No admin intervention required.
Yes — Akademia is built on a multi-tenant architecture. A single deployment can serve many schools simultaneously, each operating in complete data isolation. Students, staff, grades, and fees from School A are never visible to School B. Each school's data is scoped at the API level on every single request, making cross-school data leakage architecturally impossible.
Akademia is packaged as a native Windows desktop app via Tauri — it's a real installed application, not a web browser shortcut. Offline support for core functions like attendance and grade entry is on our near-term roadmap, with sync happening automatically when the connection is restored. This is especially important for schools in areas with unreliable internet in Ghana.
Grades are made up of three components: Continuous Assessment (CA) at 30%, Mid-term at 20%, and End-of-term Exam at 50%. Akademia computes the weighted aggregate automatically. Grade bands (e.g., A1 for 80–100, B2 for 70–79, etc.) are fully configurable by the School Admin. Teachers can only enter grades for the specific class-subject combinations they've been assigned — preventing accidental or unauthorized edits.
Akademia integrates with Paystack as the primary payment processor — which supports mobile money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money), card payments, and bank transfers for Ghanaian payers. Flutterwave is available as a secondary option. Payments are verified via webhook with a polling-based fallback to handle webhook delivery failures.
Yes — Akademia uses Arkesel as the primary SMS provider, which is built for Ghana and covers MTN, Vodafone, and AirtelTigo numbers reliably. Hubtel is available as a fallback. Phone numbers are automatically normalized to the correct format (e.g., 0244123456 → +233244123456) before sending. Every SMS dispatch is logged with its status, so you always know what was sent and to whom.
Akademia offers three plans: Basic, Standard, and Premium — each with different student capacity limits and feature access. Subscriptions are annual and managed per school (tenant). Schools that miss a renewal get a configurable grace period with read-only access before being locked out. Renewal is handled directly through Paystack from within the app itself.
Akademia is currently a Windows desktop application, launching on the Microsoft Store. A macOS version packaged via the App Store is in active development. A mobile companion app (PWA or native) for guardians to check fees, attendance, and results is on our product roadmap. The core web application runs in any modern browser as well.
We take data security seriously, especially given that student data involves minors. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (Supabase PostgreSQL with encryption). Authentication uses short-lived JWTs via Supabase Auth. Every action in the system is logged in the audit trail. Tenant isolation prevents any cross-school data access. We're aligned with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and are building toward a full data protection compliance posture.
